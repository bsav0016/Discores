Press kit

In one sentence

Discores is a free disc golf scorecard that works with no signal, keeps your rounds on your account, and lets you play with friends, with no ads and no tracking.

The short version

  • What it is: a disc golf scorecard and stats app for iPhone and Android.
  • Price: free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • Works offline: scores are saved on the phone first and sync when you are back online.
  • Friends: add friends by code, QR code, link or username. Each friend confirms their own scores in a shared round.
  • Courses: more than 3,000 courses worldwide, searchable by name or city, and you can browse them by place. Players can add courses and help confirm pars.
  • Stats: your best score at each course, how you do on every hole, and a birdie, par and bogey breakdown.
  • Privacy: no ads, no analytics, no location tracking. Rounds can be scored as a guest with no account.
  • Maker: Savidge Apps.
