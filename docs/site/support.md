# Support

Discores is made by Savidge Apps. If something is wrong or you have an idea, email **savidgeapps@gmail.com**.

## When you write, please include

- Your phone (for example "iPhone 15, iOS 19" or "Pixel 8, Android 16") and the app version if you know it (it is on the app's store page).
- What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
- The email on your account, if the problem is about your account. Never send your password.

## Common questions

- **A round says "waiting to sync".** The phone could not reach the server. It keeps trying and sends the round when you are back online. Pull down on the Rounds list, or tap the refresh button at the top, to try now.
- **I forgot my password.** On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?** A message is sent to the email on the account.
- **A friend does not see my round.** Friends confirm a finished round before it appears in their history. Ask them to open Discores and look for a round to confirm on the Rounds list.
- **A course or its pars are wrong.** Use **Report a problem** on the course, or change the pars during a round. See the [FAQ](faq.html).
- **I want my account and data deleted.** Follow [these steps](delete-account.html). If you cannot use the app, email us from the address on the account.

## Reporting someone

You can block or report any person from the Friends tab. Reports are reviewed by a person.
