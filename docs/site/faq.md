# Frequently asked questions

## Is Discores free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

## Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Every score is saved on your phone first, so you can score a whole round with no signal. When you are back online, a round you are signed in for is saved to your account.

## Do I need an account?

No. Use **Try it without an account** to score rounds as a guest; they stay on your phone. Create an account when you want your rounds saved, restored on a new phone, shared with friends and counted in your stats.

## How do I play with friends?

Add friends with a friend code, a QR code, an invite link, or their @username. When you score a round with friends who have Discores, each of them checks their own scores and confirms them, so nobody ends up with a score they did not play. Friends without the app can be added by name.

## Which phones does it run on?

iPhone and Android. Get it from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/discores-disc-golf-scorecard/id6820334635) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.discores.app).

## Where do the courses come from? Mine is missing or wrong.

Courses come from OpenStreetMap contributors and the Overture Maps Foundation, and you can [browse them by place](courses/index.html). If yours is missing, add it in the app (**Start a round**, then **Add a course**). If something is wrong, use **Report a problem** on the course. Course pars start as unconfirmed. When you change the holes or pars during a round and finish it, those pars are suggested to the course, and when two different players agree on the same pars the course is confirmed for everyone.

## Can I change the pars during a round?

Yes. On a round in progress, tap **Edit holes and pars**. Your own round keeps the pars you played it with, whatever happens to the course later.

## What do you do with my data?

Very little. We keep your account, your name, your birth year and your rounds so they come back on a new phone. We do not run ads or analytics, sell your data, or use your location. Read the full [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

## How do I delete my account or a round?

Delete a round from the round screen. Delete your account in **Settings**, then **Delete account and data**. The steps are on the [delete your account](delete-account.html) page.
